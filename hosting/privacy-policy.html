Privacy Policy

Jyoti Kirana Store app · Effective date: 26 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Jyoti Kirana Store Android app ("the app", "we", "us") collects, why, who it is shared with, and how you can have it deleted. The app is a wholesale and retail ordering app for registered retailers of Jyoti Kirana Store in India.

1. Information we collect

WhatWhenWhy
Name, shop name, email address, phone number, passwordWhen you sign upTo create your account, let the store owner approve it, and contact you about orders. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see it.
Delivery addresses (street, city, PIN code) and, if you tap "Use current location", your precise GPS locationWhen you add an address or place an orderTo deliver your order and calculate the delivery charge, which depends on distance. Location is read only when you tap the button, never in the background.
GST number, business hoursOptional, in your profileTo show on your invoices and help the store serve your shop.
Bank or UPI payout details (account holder, account number, IFSC, bank name, UPI ID)Optional, in your profileOnly for refunds or payouts from the store to you.
Profile photo and UPI payment screenshotsOnly when you choose an image from your galleryA profile photo for your account; a payment screenshot so the store can confirm your UPI payment.
Orders: products, quantities, amounts, payment method and status, coupon usedWhen you place an orderTo process, deliver and invoice your order, and to show your order history.
Device push-notification tokenAutomatically after sign-inTo send you order updates and store announcements. You can turn notification types off in Settings.
App usage events (sign-in, sign-up, order placed) and crash reportsAutomaticallyTo understand how the app is used and fix crashes. These do not include your name, address or payment details.

We do not collect your contacts, SMS, call logs, or files other than the images you choose. We do not process card or UPI payments inside the app — UPI payments happen in your own UPI app.

2. How we use it

Only to run the store: approving retailer accounts, taking and delivering orders, calculating delivery charges, issuing invoices, sending order updates and store announcements, giving support, and keeping the app working. We do not sell your information and do not use it for advertising.

3. Who we share it with

  • The store owner (admin) sees your account details, addresses and orders to approve your account and fulfil orders.
  • Service providers that store or process data for us:
    • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore database, Cloud Messaging, Analytics, Crashlytics, App Check) — Firebase privacy
    • Cloudinary — stores profile photos, product images and payment screenshots — Cloudinary privacy
    • Your phone's map/geocoding service (Google on most Android phones) — turns your GPS location into a readable address
  • Authorities, only when required by law.

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Firestore data is stored in Google Cloud's Mumbai region (asia-south1).

4. How long we keep it

  • Account and profile details: until you delete your account.
  • Orders and invoices: kept for up to 8 years after the order, as Indian tax and accounting law requires business records to be retained.
  • Crash reports and usage events: kept by Firebase for its standard periods (crash data about 90 days; analytics up to 14 months).
  • Notification history is stored only on your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app.

5. Deleting your account and data

In the app: open Profile → Settings → Delete account, confirm with your password. This immediately deletes your login and your profile (name, shop name, phone, addresses, GST number, payout details, preferences).

Without the app: email vishvatejkatkar007@gmail.com from the email address you registered with, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete your account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

You can also ask us by email to delete your profile photo and payment screenshots. Past orders and invoices are kept for the period in section 4 because the law requires it; after that they are deleted.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile, addresses and payout details at any time in the app.
  • Turn order-update, stock-alert and promotional notifications on or off in Settings.
  • Deny or withdraw location permission in Android settings — you can still type your address by hand.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion, using the contact details below. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

7. Children

The app is for business customers (retailers) and is not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
